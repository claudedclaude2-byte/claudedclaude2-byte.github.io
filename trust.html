Trust & scope

Keep the source.
Show the limits.

Traceability makes a finding easier to inspect. It does not make that finding automatically true, complete or legally admissible.

Working principles

Preserve sourcesBounded checksHuman reviewData & access

Preserve sources and revisions

Keep originals, changes and reasons inspectable. Attach claims to sources so a reviewer can see the passage and its location. A result belongs to the version and environment that produced it.

Missing, unread and not-yet-reviewed states are meaningful information. Unresolved contradictions should remain visible.

State what was checked

A package verifier may check structure, references, fingerprints, revisions and supported citation locations. A text match describes a defined comparison.

These mechanical results do not establish truth, source authenticity, lawful acquisition, completeness, legal admissibility or genuine human approval.

Keep judgment human

People assess meaning, context, accuracy and approval. Individually reviewed redaction derivatives preserve their originals. Identity review and contradiction flags are conservative and attributable.

Optional extraction or development tools do not turn a mechanical check into a factual decision. Semantic/vector search is deferred.

Respect the actual access boundary

This is a public, static project website. It has no record upload, case-processing feature or hosted product account. The implementation repository and investigation records remain private.

The designed example on the homepage uses fictional content. For a first conversation, describe a workflow and leave out private records, client information, credentials and operational databases.

The local-first direction does not imply that every optional tool is offline. External extraction tools and dependencies must be assessed within the actual workflow.
