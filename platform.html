The implemented foundations

Records requests & responses

Request history, versioned drafting, deadline helpers, responses, expected-record comparisons, gaps and follow-up recommendations. Live sending requires human authorization.

Evidence catalog & search

Supported package indexing, fingerprints, extraction outcomes, full-text search and structured or entity-aware filters. Optional extraction tools have their own requirements.

Provenance & claims

On-demand source snapshots, evidence-anchored claims, conservative identity review and attributable contradiction flags.

Redaction & dossiers

Individually reviewed derivatives, preserved originals, revisioned dossiers, supported passage citations and independent offline package checks.

The bounded PDF pilot

One factual question, up to six born-digital PDFs, twenty pages total and ten pages per document revision, with one correction round. The operator supplies statements and expected passages.

The draft review pack distinguishes exact matches, possible matches, missing records and unread files. Optional scans need individually reviewed transcripts. This is a focused preparation and checking workflow; factual judgment remains human.

Final integration and clean-machine acceptance remain open. A public runnable release is not yet available.

Two local interfaces

A read-only investigation Workspace presents broader project information. A separate bounded PDF pilot supports intake, preparation, one correction round and review. Their purposes and requirements are distinct.

The core direction uses Python and SQLite/FTS5 with local Flask/Jinja interfaces. Semantic/vector search, multi-user operation and hosted product access remain outside the currently offered scope.
