A finding is only useful
if you can inspect it.

A folder of records can hide missing attachments, competing statements and corrected versions. The project connects those pieces into a reviewable trail.

How verification is bounded

Find the supporting passage

Keep the document and page location beside the finding, with explicit outcomes for exact matches, possible matches and unresolved gaps.

Keep the history inspectable

Preserve originals and corrections. A result belongs to the revision that produced it.

Make the review decision visible

Separate a mechanical check from a person's assessment. Missing and not-yet-reviewed information stays explicit.
